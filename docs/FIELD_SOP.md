# Community Outreach Intelligence Desk

## Standard Operating Procedure for Outreach Days

**ISEYC × Tirngan Sickle Cell Foundation**  
Data stewardship: Miracle Sim Danjuma, Founder, Tirngan Sickle Cell Foundation

---

### Before Outreach

1. **Confirm access**
   - Open the Community Outreach Intelligence Desk.
   - Enter the authorised staff passcode if requested.
   - Confirm internet connectivity where possible.

2. **Start the outreach session**  
   Select **+ Start new outreach**. Enter:
   - Programme
   - Date
   - State
   - LGA
   - Ward (optional)
   - Community
   - Outreach type
   - Field lead
   - Team size

3. **Check session details**  
   Confirm location and programme information before collecting responses.

---

### During Outreach

4. **Explain the purpose**  
   Briefly explain why information is being collected and allow the respondent to make an informed choice.

5. **Confirm consent**  
   Proceed only after the respondent agrees to participate.

6. **Capture one response at a time**  
   Follow the six-step form in order. Ask the questions as presented. Do not add personal interpretations or alter the meaning of the questions.

7. **Review before submitting**  
   Check that key responses are correctly recorded.

8. **Save**  
   Select **Save & Add Next Person**. A successful submission takes you directly to the next response.

---

### If Connectivity Is Poor

- Do not repeatedly submit the same response.
- If the desk indicates that the response is saved for later synchronisation, continue with the outreach.
- Confirm the response is recorded before moving on.

---

### Finishing the Outreach

9. Complete the remaining responses.
10. Select **Finish** when the outreach is complete.
11. Where connectivity permits, ensure outstanding responses have synchronised.

---

### When to Use Insights

Field workers should primarily use Insights after an outreach session, unless their role specifically includes programme monitoring.

Programme managers and the Founder can use Insights to review:
- total responses;
- communities reached;
- major reported barriers;
- indicator percentages;
- LGA/community differences; and
- emerging programme priorities.

**Important:** Insights are for understanding patterns and planning programmes. They should not be presented as individual medical assessments.

---

### Founder Evidence Brief

Miracle Sim Danjuma (and authorised programme managers) can open **Insights → Founder Evidence Brief** to:
- Print / PDF for meetings and proposals
- Copy the summary into email or partner communications

Evidence always shows sample size (numerator / denominator).
