Dear Miracle,

The ISEYC × TIRNGAN Community Outreach Intelligence Desk has been established as a shared digital platform for capturing structured information from community outreach activities and turning it into useful programme evidence.

The platform is designed to help the partnership understand recurring community barriers across locations — including access to medication, affordability of investigations, genotype knowledge and travel/access challenges — without creating clinical records.

As Founder of Tirngan Sickle Cell Foundation, you remain the designated data steward for the programme information collected through the desk. The platform is intended to give you direct visibility of the evidence generated through outreach.

To review the evidence, open the **Insights** section and select **Founder Evidence Brief**. From there, you can:

- **Print / PDF** to produce a professional copy for meetings, records or proposals.
- **Copy** to transfer the evidence into an email, proposal or briefing document.

You do not need to understand the technical backend to use the evidence. The desk is designed so that the important information is presented in a clear, decision-ready format.

ISEYC provides the digital infrastructure and technical support; Tirngan retains programme stewardship of the evidence.

With respect,  
ISEYC Technical Partnership Team
