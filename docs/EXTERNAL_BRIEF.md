# Community Outreach Evidence for Better Health-Equity Programmes

**ISEYC × Tirngan Sickle Cell Foundation**

## Who We Are

Initiative for Sustainable Evolution for Youth and Community (ISEYC) and Tirngan Sickle Cell Foundation are working together to strengthen community outreach through structured, privacy-conscious data collection.

The partnership combines ISEYC’s systems and digital capacity with Tirngan’s community-focused work and programme experience under the leadership of **Miracle Sim Danjuma**, Founder, Tirngan Sickle Cell Foundation.

---

## Why Community Barrier Data Matters

Community outreach should do more than record attendance.

Structured community information helps partners understand:

- where people experience the greatest barriers;
- which challenges occur most frequently;
- how needs differ between communities and LGAs;
- where future outreach and support should be prioritised; and
- what evidence can inform engagement with government, healthcare providers and development partners.

The objective is to move from activity records to useful community evidence.

---

## What We Measure

The Outreach Intelligence Desk focuses on four core indicators:

1. **Medication Access** — How easily respondents report being able to access needed medication.
2. **Investigation Affordability** — Whether the cost of investigations creates a reported barrier.
3. **Genotype Knowledge** — Whether respondents know and understand their genotype information.
4. **Travel & Access** — The time and practical difficulty involved in reaching relevant services.

The system also captures broader support needs and reported barriers to help guide programme planning.

---

## How Privacy Is Protected

The desk is designed around data minimisation and purpose limitation.

We do not use the platform to create clinical records or collect medical diagnoses. Information is collected specifically to understand community-level barriers and programme needs.

Evidence is primarily presented in aggregate so that partners can understand patterns without unnecessary exposure of individual information.
