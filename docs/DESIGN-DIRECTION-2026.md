# From Girlhood to Greatness — Design Direction

## Purpose
Redesign the existing event platform as a distinct, editorial-led experience for the second edition of From Girlhood to Greatness. Preserve the existing Next.js application, registration API, Notion integration, staff access and operational workflows.

## Brand facts
- Official organiser identity: Empower with Hafsat.
- The supplied circular blush-pink logo is the authoritative brand mark. Use the original artwork as an image asset; never redraw it, approximate it with CSS, or use a pseudo-element as a logo.
- Event: From Girlhood to Greatness — Second Edition.
- Theme: Accepting My Greatness — From Becoming Her to Believing in Her.
- Date and time: Sunday, 29 November 2026, 1:00 PM.
- Venue: Yakubu Hub, Surami Road, U/Rimi, Kaduna, Nigeria.
- Audience: women aged 20 and above.
- Organiser: Empower with Hafsat.
- In partnership with: Baytal Rihaa.
- Registration and documentation systems partner: ISEYC.

## Design principles drawn from the supplied references
1. Anthropic Frontend Design: make the visual language specific to the subject and audience; plan typography, palette, composition and layout before coding; use one memorable visual gesture and remove generic decoration.
2. Taste Skill: use real, subject-appropriate photography, a coherent system, clear section pacing and quiet interactions. Avoid generic cards, fake product UI, gratuitous shaders and ornamental scroll effects.
3. Impeccable: use a deliberate critique-and-refinement workflow rather than surface-level beautification.
4. UI/UX Pro Max: evaluate page patterns, responsive behaviour, component hierarchy, accessibility and consistency together.
5. Emil Kowalski / Design Engineering: motion should clarify state and interaction, remain restrained, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
6. Vercel Web Design Guidelines: review semantic structure, keyboard interaction, focus, responsive layout, loading and performance.
7. Better UI: refine typography, colour, spacing, optical alignment, surfaces, icons and motion details.
8. Refero, Awwwards, Godly, Land-book, ThinkUI and 60FPS: use as reference libraries for research and comparison, never as sources to copy a signature layout or effect.

## Chosen direction
- Editorial portrait photography is the hero's main visual, rather than an abstract CSS illustration.
- The supplied logo is a real image in the header, registration context, organiser lockup and footer.
- Blush is grounded in the supplied mark; plum and cocoa provide legible contrast; muted gold is a restrained detail.
- Serif display typography gives the event a human, editorial voice; a clean sans-serif supports navigation and forms.
- Keep the hero focused on the event, the date, the venue and the registration action.
- Keep event details scannable and the registration form calm, accessible and mobile-first.
- Staff operations should share the identity but retain the density and clarity required for real event-day work.

## Asset provenance
The hero campaign image was generated for this project with Higgsfield Soul 2.0. It is campaign photography only; it does not contain or replace the official logo. The original logo remains at `public/brand/empower-with-hafsat.jpg`.

## Quality gates
- Verify the logo loads as a real image on desktop and mobile.
- Verify the generated hero image loads and has useful cropping at mobile and desktop widths.
- Check heading hierarchy, colour contrast, keyboard focus, form labels and error/success states.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Do not remove or mock registration, tracking, Notion persistence, feedback, authentication or check-in.
- Do not deploy to production or merge the pull request without explicit approval.
